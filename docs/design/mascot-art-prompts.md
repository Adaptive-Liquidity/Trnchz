# Trenchz Mascot Art Prompt Pack

Use these prompts for the next asset generation pass once `OPENAI_API_KEY` and the image generation CLI are available. The goal is meme-native mascot art with premium collectible finish, not comedy slop or generic mobile-game rendering.

## Direction

- Style target: BAYC / MAYC adjacent premium mascot collectible energy
- Product context: dark tactical Solana survival arena
- Tone: dangerous, smug, expensive, battle-tested
- Palette: toxic green, ultraviolet, ember orange, obsidian, cold steel
- Avoid: flat cartoon children’s-book look, cheap sticker-pack vibe, glossy stock-game UI, too many random props, washed-out daylight scenes
